## 📅 Weekly News · Top 10

> **Updated 2026-11-03** · Weekly news picks, refreshed every Monday alongside citation rankings.
>
> **How we select:** The top stories from the trailing 30 days, scored on publisher authority, discussion volume, whether a concrete verifiable result is reported, whether it changes what agent builders do now, whether an artifact was released, and expected durability. Only primary sources are cited, and performance claims remain attributed to their publishers.

| Date · Type | News | Why it matters |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 2026‑11‑02<br><sub>Paper</sub> | [**SelfImprove-Bench: Evaluating Generalization in Self-Improving Agents**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02797)<br><sub>Xu, Yang et al. · arXiv</sub> | The authors introduce a reproducible RSI benchmark with a G0–G4 generalization ladder spanning exploration, planning and reasoning, and report that evolution gains do not consistently transfer across levels. |
| 2026‑10‑30<br><sub>Paper</sub> | [**Exo: An Architecture for Self-Improving Systems at Runtime**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00463)<br><sub>Krentsel et al. · arXiv</sub> | The authors let an agent revise its harness during ongoing work behind a protected activation layer. Against a fixed-policy control, they report 35–61% lower held-out model cost on AppWorld and SREGym with comparable correctness. |
| 2026‑10‑29<br><sub>Paper</sub> | [**MoLRSI: A Recursive Self-Improving AI agent for Molecular Discovery**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39714)<br><sub>Zhang et al. · arXiv</sub> | The authors combine workflow and model-architecture search with persistent cross-task memory. They report a 15.9% experimental antibiotic hit rate versus 8.7% for a prior expert-led workflow on the same training data and screening library. |
| 2026‑10‑28<br><sub>Model release</sub> | [**Gemini 4 Carbon: our next frontier model**](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/)<br><sub>Google DeepMind</sub> | Google announces Carbon for long-horizon engineering and cybersecurity, reporting 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro. Access begins with pre-release testing and selected cyber defenders through Fairwind; general developer and consumer access is still forthcoming. |
| 2026‑10‑26<br><sub>Model release</sub> | [**Introducing GPT-6.1 Terra**](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra/)<br><sub>OpenAI</sub> | OpenAI releases a model for visual reasoning and computer use in Work, Codex and the API. It reports a 15-point Agents’ Last Exam gain over GPT-5.6 Terra at medium effort at about one-third the cost, and 50.3% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 at max effort. |
| 2026‑10‑26<br><sub>Paper</sub> | [**Skill∞: Recursive Self-Improvement for Agent Skill Optimization**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35434)<br><sub>Tao et al. · arXiv</sub> | The authors make the skill optimizer itself a reusable, revisable meta-skill. They report 60.7% on 83 SkillsBench tasks, compared with 56.4% for human-curated skills and 54.5% for SkillEvolver. |
| 2026‑10‑23<br><sub>Model release</sub> | [**Kimi K3.1 Pro series is live**](https://www.kimi.com/code/docs/en/kimi-code/whats-new.html)<br><sub>Moonshot AI</sub> | Moonshot rolls out Kimi K3.1 Pro and its Highspeed variant in Kimi Code, with a 1M-token context window and improved long-horizon agent capabilities. Its technical blog reports 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and says full weights are planned by November 4; this edition treats the product launch and future weight release separately. |
| 2026‑10‑18<br><sub>Open-weight release</sub> | [**Introducing Flint**](https://recursive.com/updates/introducing-recursive-flint)<br><sub>Recursive</sub> | Recursive releases a 40B-total / 2.5B-active MoE for local agentic coding and says its Eureka Machine helped develop the architecture, data, training, quantization and inference stack. The official post reports over 125 tokens/s on an M4 Pro at short contexts. |
| 2026‑10‑16<br><sub>Open-weight release</sub> | [**Beam is now available**](https://reflection.ai/blog/beam-is-available)<br><sub>Reflection</sub> | Reflection releases Beam under Apache 2.0 with 501B total and 23B active parameters, multiple weight precisions, a speculative drafter, and the Mirror CLI harness. The release makes its coding and long-horizon agent stack available for deployment and customization. |
| 2026‑10‑09<br><sub>Paper</sub> | [**Harness²: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224)<br><sub>Google Research</sub> | Google Research introduces two connected loops that refine the current task harness and retain evidence-supported lessons for future tasks, with model weights fixed. The authors report gains across three professional benchmarks; the Apache-2.0 implementation supports OpenCode and Codex, parallel and sequential recursion, and reproducible benchmark runs. [Official code](https://github.com/google-research/harness2). |
