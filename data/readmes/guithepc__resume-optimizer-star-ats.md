# resume-optimizer-star-ats

A Claude skill that rewrites your resume bullet points using the **STAR** and **XYZ** frameworks, optimizes your About/Summary section, and runs an **ATS keyword audit** based on what the tech job market actually searches for.

## What it does

- Rewrites weak bullet points into results-driven statements using STAR and XYZ
- Never fabricates metrics — asks you first, then helps you quantify
- Detects your domain (Backend, Frontend, DevOps, Data, Cyber, Mobile) and audits ATS keywords accordingly
- Rewrites your About/Summary to be punchy and keyword-rich
- Outputs formatted `.tex` with bold keywords and results for LaTeX resumes
- Spots undervalued projects in your CV and helps you extract the real impact

## Install

```bash
npx skills add guithepc/resume-optimizer-star-ats
```

## Usage

Just paste your resume bullets, your About section, or your full CV into the chat. The skill will take it from there.
