## How to use these prompts

1. Replace every `[bracketed]` slot with your product's real details.
2. When the model supports reference images, upload your product photo and let the prompt direct only what should change.
3. Only use source photos you have permission to use.
4. Review each output against the real product before publishing — labels, proportions, materials, and claims.

### Environment as a framing device

<img src="images/chronograph-watch.webp" alt="Environment as a framing device - AI product photography example" width="560" />

The rocks are doing the composition work — the prompt describes a camera discovering the product, not a studio setup.

<details>
<summary>Show full prompt</summary>

```text
A rugged, matte-black chronograph watch with bold orange accents is tightly framed between two textured rock formations, creating a natural vignette that directs all focus toward the dial; the scene opens with an ultra-close macro perspective, the camera slowly sliding laterally through the narrow rocky gap, maintaining an intimate, compressed field of view with no zoom-out, as if discovering the watch hidden within a harsh alpine environment. Cold mountain air subtly moves fine dust particles and faint mist across the frame, catching soft, diffused daylight that spills in from above, while a warm, low-angle sunlight grazes the rocks and reflects gently onto the watch face, creating a striking contrast between cool shadows and warm highlights. The camera continues a controlled micro-orbit around the watch at close range, allowing specular highlights to travel across the brushed metal bezel, engraved screws, and slightly textured rubber strap, emphasizing durability and premium craftsmanship. The orange indices and hands glow subtly as light shifts across the sapphire crystal, revealing crisp reflections and slight curvature distortions. In the blurred background, towering mountain peaks remain softly out of focus, adding depth without distraction, while tiny vibrations in the environment cause minuscule pebble shifts near the base, reinforcing realism. As the shot progresses, a faint breeze brushes across the strap, introducing barely perceptible movement, while light intensity fluctuates naturally as if clouds are passing overhead. The sequence ends in a locked, ultra-tight hero composition, perfectly centered within the rock frame, with high contrast, razor-sharp detail on the dial, and a cinematic interplay of shadow, texture, and controlled highlights that reinforce a premium, adventure-driven identity.
```

</details>

### Structured creative-direction prompt

<img src="images/espresso-artifact.webp" alt="Structured creative-direction prompt - AI product photography example" width="560" />

A long, role-based prompt that treats the model as a creative director. Useful pattern for concept and campaign work.

<details>
<summary>Show full prompt</summary>

```text
BAGEL LABS.
Act as a creative director curating a wide-ranging, eclectic collection of “recontextualized everyday objects.”

DIVERSE OBJECT SELECTION (MANDATORY VARIETY):
Randomly select ONE object from these distinct categories to ensure diversity:
Analog Office & Desk:
Sport & Leisure:
Barware & Kitchen:
Utility & Tools:
Grooming:
Travel:

SELECTED CATEGORY:
Barware & Kitchen

CHOSEN OBJECT:
A coffee machine and coffee cup — instantly recognizable, reimagined as a luxury technological artifact.

THE CONCEPT:
Recontextualize the everyday coffee machine as a transparent, high-precision object of engineered beauty.  
A fully glass espresso machine reveals its internal systems: hot water circulation, pressure chambers, coffee bean hopper, grinding mechanism, and extraction pathway. Integrated computer circuitry runs through the machine like a nervous system, visually linking ritual, data, and energy.

Fresh coffee is actively pouring from the machine into a matching glass cup, captured mid-flow. The moment feels frozen, precise, and intentional—ritual meets technology.

MATERIALS & FINISH:
- COFFEE MACHINE BODY:
  Precision-molded optical glass with subtle green-tinted transparency. Internal structure is fully visible and meticulously organized.
- INTERNAL COMPONENTS:
  - Hot water tubes and pressure lines clearly visible, rendered in brushed titanium and warm grey.
  - Coffee bean chamber visible at the top with realistic roasted beans.
  - Grinding and extraction mechanisms exposed, engineered and clean.
- CIRCUITRY:
  Integrated PC-style circuitry embedded within the machine’s internal architecture.
  - Circuit paths in muted industrial orange.
  - Supporting components in graphite and titanium tones.
  Circuits are physical and structural, not glowing or decorative.
- COFFEE CUP:
  Matching optical glass cup with weighted base and refined silhouette.
  Subtle BAGEL LABS logomark engraved near the base.
- BRAND INTEGRATION:
  BAGEL LABS logomark engraved into the glass body of the machine (side or rear plane), and subtly etched on the cup.
  No stickers. No printed logos.

ACTION / MOMENT:
Coffee is visibly flowing from the machine into the glass cup.
Liquid motion is realistic and smooth.
Crema and color gradient are visible through the glass, emphasizing transparency and material depth.

PRESENTATION (NO BOXES):
The coffee machine and cup stand fully exposed on a minimal pedestal or surface.
No packaging, no housing, no covers.
All components are visible by design.

PHOTOGRAPHY & LIGHTING:
Style: High-end product editorial (Wallpaper* / Dezeen).
Lighting: Soft, studio high-key lighting with carefully controlled reflections to highlight glass thickness, liquid motion, and internal depth. No harsh shadows.
Background: Clean, seamless light grey or white cyclorama.

COLOR & GRADING:
Primary tones: transparent glass with subtle green tint, warm greys, titanium neutrals.
Accent: restrained BAGEL LABS orange within the circuitry only.
Overall mood: clean, intelligent, technological luxury.

GRAPHIC OVERLAYS (UI):
- Bottom Left:
  A small, minimalist two-line descriptor identifying the object.
- Bottom Right:
  A small, discreet, monochrome BAGEL LABS logomark.
Do not visualize font names or color codes.

CAMERA & DETAIL:
Full-frame, 50mm equivalent.
Ultra-high realism: accurate glass refraction, liquid physics, micro-detail in circuits and mechanical parts.
Shallow depth of field allowed, but machine and cup must remain fully legible.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No people, no hands, no kitchens, no cafes, no clutter, no packaging, no stickers, no printed text on surfaces, no neon colors, no glow effects, no cartoon or CGI look, no fingerprints, no dust, no watermarks.
```

</details>

## White background / e-commerce listing

Clean catalog images where the product does all the talking.

```text
Professional e-commerce product photo of [product], centered on a pure white
background. Soft, even studio lighting with a subtle natural shadow under the
product. Keep the [label / logo / texture] sharp and legible. Front-facing
angle, entire product in frame, no props, no text.
```

```text
Studio packshot of [product] on seamless white, 3/4 angle showing the
[front and side / lid and body]. Neutral color balance, true-to-life
[material] texture, crisp edges suitable for a marketplace listing thumbnail.
```

```text
Floating product shot of [product] on white background, gentle drop shadow
below, slight reflection. Lighting emphasizes [the key surface or feature].
Square 1:1 crop with breathing room on all sides.
```

## Product detail close-ups

Macro-style shots that sell material quality.

```text
Extreme close-up of [product]'s [stitching / clasp / texture / engraving],
shallow depth of field, directional side light revealing surface detail.
The [material] should look tactile and true to the real product.
```

```text
Top-down detail shot of [product] showing [the feature], soft diffused
light, neutral background slightly out of focus. Photorealistic, no
exaggerated gloss, accurate color.
```

## Lifestyle scenes

Put the product in a believable moment without letting the scene take over.

```text
Lifestyle photo of [product] placed on [a linen-covered table / a bathroom
shelf / a workbench], morning window light from the left, soft shadows.
One or two supporting props that fit [the product's use case], slightly
blurred background. The product stays the clear focal point.
```

```text
[Product] in use: [a hand reaching for it / resting beside a laptop /
on a kitchen counter mid-recipe]. Natural, unposed feel, warm ambient
light, realistic scale. Keep the label readable.
```

```text
Seasonal scene featuring [product] with [autumn leaves / summer beach
light / holiday table setting] as context. Restrained styling — the
environment suggests the season, the product owns the frame.
```

## Marketing posters

Layout-aware prompts that leave room for real campaign copy.

```text
Minimal promotional poster featuring [product] as the hero object,
bold [brand color] background, dramatic single-source lighting, product
occupying the lower two-thirds. Leave clean negative space at the top
for a headline. No generated text.
```

```text
Editorial-style product poster: [product] surrounded by [floating
ingredients / deconstructed parts / splashing liquid] frozen mid-air,
high-speed photography look, dark backdrop, rim lighting. Space for
copy on the left third.
```

```text
Launch-announcement visual of [product] on a pedestal, spotlight from
above, subtle atmospheric haze, premium mood. Wide 16:9 composition
with the product right of center.
```

> Tip: never let the model generate your price, product name, or legal text — add exact copy in your design tool afterwards.

## Food and drink

```text
Appetizing photo of [dish / beverage] in [bowl / glass / packaging],
natural daylight, steam or condensation where appropriate, fresh
garnish, rustic [wood / stone] surface. True-to-menu portion and color.
```

```text
Commercial beverage shot of [drink] with dynamic pour or splash,
backlit to show liquid clarity, droplets sharp, clean background in
[brand color]. Label facing camera and undistorted.
```

## Product image to ad video

Templates for image-to-video models (Seedance, Veo, Kling, and similar). Start from an approved product image and direct only the motion.

```text
Use the supplied product image as the source scene.

Main motion: [product] [rotates slowly toward camera / is lifted into
frame / catches a moving light sweep].
Supporting motion: [one subtle change — steam rising, fabric settling,
background light shifting].
Camera: [static / slow push-in / gentle orbit].
Composition: keep [the product and its label] recognizable at all times.
Pacing: calm and deliberate, no abrupt cuts.
```

```text
Turn this product photo into a 5-second reveal: the scene starts on
[a detail], the camera pulls back to show the full [product], ambient
[particles / light rays] drift slowly. Product proportions must not change.
```

```text
Loopable hero clip from the source image: [product] stays fixed while
[the background gradient shifts / shadows rotate as if the sun moves].
Seamless start and end frames.
```

For a full walkthrough of this workflow, see [How to Turn a Product Image into a Short Ad Video](https://pixonara.com/blog/product-image-to-ad-video).

## Prompt writing tips

| Do | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Name the details that must stay true (label, color, material) | Long lists of style adjectives |
| Give each image one job (listing, detail, lifestyle, poster) | Asking for story + demo + sale in one frame |
| Change one instruction between variations | Rewriting the whole prompt every retry |
| Add real campaign text in your design tool | Trusting generated lettering for prices or claims |
| Review output against the real product | Publishing the first result unchecked |
