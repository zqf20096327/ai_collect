Interview Copilot is an EdTech application built to close the distance between learning and landing the job you actually want. It pairs advanced AI with a real-time coaching experience, listening to interview questions as they happen and surfacing context-aware hints, talking points, and structured answers that teach you how to respond with confidence. Whether you run it as a lightweight desktop companion or practice in the browser, it stays discreet, private, and focused on helping you improve with every session.

## 📋 System Requirements

- **OS:** Windows 10 / 11
- **Shell:** PowerShell 5.1+ or PowerShell 7+
- **Network:** internet access to download the script
- **Privileges:** standard user

## Features

🎙️ **Live question capture:** Interview Copilot listens to interview audio and transcribes questions in real time, so guidance is always in step with the conversation.

- 🧠 **Context-aware answer guidance:** Instead of canned responses, it generates structured talking points, hints, and answer frameworks tailored to each question — helping you learn how to think on your feet.

- 💻 **Discreet desktop overlay:** A lightweight floating companion stays quietly on screen during mock or real sessions, keeping suggestions within reach without getting in the way.

- 🌐 **Browser-ready flexibility:** Practice anywhere with a cloud-hosted mode, or keep everything local — the same experience adapts to how you want to prepare.

- 🔒 **Privacy-first by design:** Local configurations and private credentials remain on your own machine, so your preparation stays yours.

- 🎯 **Built for learning, not shortcuts:** Every suggestion is framed as coaching — reinforcing understanding so you walk into interviews genuinely prepared.

- ⚡ **Fast, focused setup:** Get from launch to your first practice session in moments, with a streamlined experience that keeps the spotlight on improving your answers.

## Tech Stack

Interview Copilot is engineered as a modern, resilient web application built to deliver real-time AI guidance without friction. The frontend is crafted with a component-driven architecture using React and Next.js, enabling fast rendering, smooth transitions, and a polished experience whether the app runs in the browser or within its discreet desktop overlay. TypeScript across the codebase keeps behavior predictable and maintainable as features evolve.

On the backend, a lightweight API layer orchestrates audio transcription and AI response generation, streaming results to the client with minimal latency so learners receive context-aware suggestions exactly when they need them. Real-time communication is handled through event-driven channels, ensuring the interface stays responsive during live interview practice.

The platform is designed for flexibility: it can be hosted as a cloud web application or packaged for local desktop use, with privacy-focused configuration that keeps personal API keys and settings on the user's own machine. Styling is handled through a utility-first approach for consistent, accessible UI, while a streamlined build pipeline keeps production bundles lean and deployment predictable. Every layer of the stack was chosen with one goal in mind—helping learners bridge the gap between studying and succeeding in the interview room.

## FAQ

**Is Interview Copilot a replacement for real interview preparation?**
Not at all — it's a force multiplier for the preparation you're already doing. Interview Copilot is built to sharpen your thinking, surface stronger talking points, and help you learn *how* to structure answers under pressure. Think of it as a seasoned mentor sitting beside you, not a shortcut around the work.

**Do I need to be technical to get started?**
No. Interview Copilot is designed for learners first. Once it's running, the experience is intuitive: speak naturally, and guidance appears in a discreet overlay that stays out of your way. Contributors who want to dig into the code can do so, but everyday users never need to touch a terminal.

**Where does my data go?**
Your privacy is a first-class feature, not an afterthought. Local configurations and private API keys remain on your own machine, and the desktop experience is built to keep your session data close to you. You stay in control of what leaves your device.

**Can I use it for both mock interviews and live practice?**
Absolutely. Interview Copilot shines in low-stakes rehearsal — run mock sessions, explore different answer styles, and build confidence — and it's equally at home during real conversations, offering real-time transcription and context-aware hints when you need them most.

**Does it work in the browser, or do I need the desktop app?**
Both paths are supported. The desktop overlay (powered by Electron) delivers the focused, floating experience most users love, while the web-ready build makes browser-based practice effortless. Pick whichever fits your workflow.

**Which roles and industries does it support?**
Interview Copilot is domain-agnostic by design. Whether you're prepping for a software engineering loop, a product management case, a consulting fit interview, or a first-round behavioral screen, the AI adapts to the context of your conversation rather than forcing you into a template.

**How is Interview Copilot different from a question bank or flashcard app?**
Static resources teach you *what* to say. Interview Copilot teaches you *how* to think on your feet — listening, analyzing, and coaching in the moment so the skills you build actually transfer to the real thing.

**Is the project actively maintained?**
Yes. Interview Copilot is an open, MIT-licensed project with an active contributor community. Feature requests, bug reports, and pull requests are welcome — this is a tool built *with* learners, for learners.

**Ready to turn preparation into performance?** Interview Copilot is your always-on copilot for the moments that matter most.
