## Practical Knowledge: 5 Rules for Google Cloud & MCP Agent Billing

### 1. Align US-Issued Card BINs with North American Cloud Gateways
Google Cloud and Workspace billing consoles check issuer BIN region, IP consistency, and billing address alignment closely. **CoreGold provides US-issued card BINs** tailored for cloud services, AI tools, and Google Pay / Google Play scenarios, helping builders clear initial billing setup smoothly.

### 2. Match AVS Billing Details Verbatim
When setting up a Google Payments profile, ensure your street address, city, state, and 5-digit ZIP code match your CoreGold card record (whether auto-generated or entered manually) verbatim to prevent compliance holds.

### 3. Cap Automated CLI & MCP Spend via Dedicated Card Balances
Automated CLI scripts and MCP workflows can trigger rapid API calls if a loop stalls. Issue a dedicated card inside CoreGold **Card Management** loaded strictly with your sprint budget so downstream charges stop automatically once the balance is spent.

### 4. Bind to Google Pay Across All CoreGold Card BINs
All CoreGold card BINs can be bound to **Google Pay** (as well as **Apple Pay**), streamlining mobile and web checkouts across supported Google ecosystem services.

### 5. Buffer $3–$5 Above Initial Charges for Pre-Authorizations
Google typically runs a $1 pre-authorization check when verifying a new card. Including a small buffer ensures smooth first-time verification.

> **Card Tip**: When binding a CoreGold card to Google Wallet or Apple Wallet, select **email verification** first, then open your card details page in CoreGold and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code immediately.

## Simple Step-by-Step Setup Tutorial

### Step 1: Register on CoreGold
Visit [https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b](https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b), register using your regular email address, and log in to the user dashboard.

### Step 2: Deposit USDT via TRON (TRC20)
1. In the left menu, click **My Wallet (我的钱包)** -> **Deposit (充值)**.
2. Enter your deposit amount to display the USDT deposit address and QR code.
3. Open your crypto wallet or exchange (such as OKX or Binance; if you don't hold USDT yet, you can purchase it there first), select USDT withdrawal/transfer, and choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
4. Copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, verify the address, network, and amount, and confirm the transfer. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact customer support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### Step 3: Select Plan, Card Category & Billing Details
1. Click **Apply for Card (申请开卡)** in the left menu and choose an **Individual Plan** (such as Diamond Plan or AI Subscription) or **Team Plan** (check live page details for current pricing, supported scenarios, and fees).
2. Pick the matching US-issued card BIN category (including cloud services, AI tools, and Google Pay / Google Play) and enter the initial deposit amount.
3. Check **Auto-Generate Billing Info** or uncheck it to enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keeping billing addresses consistent during merchant checkout.

### Step 4: View Card & Bind Google Pay / Apple Pay
1. Open **Card Management (卡片管理)** and select your card to view card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, or use top-up, withdrawal, and freeze controls.
2. To link **Google Pay** or **Apple Pay**:
   - Open **Google Wallet** on Android or **Apple Wallet** on iPhone, select **Add Payment Card**, and enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually.
   - Choose **email verification** when prompted.
   - Return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code.
   - Enter the verification code back into your mobile wallet to complete binding.
