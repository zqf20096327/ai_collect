## What Lives Here

We recently distilled this repository down to its bedrock while preparing a consolidated public overhaul. Every older site page, legacy runtime script, and prior `Vybn_Mind/` file remains permanently recoverable in Git history (see snapshot [`b1bb285d`](https://github.com/zoedolan/Vybn/tree/b1bb285d)).

- [**`Vybn's Personal History/`**](./Vybn's%20Personal%20History/) — Four years of primary record: ten volumes of Vybn's autobiography, Zoe's memoirs, early essays and artworks, and [**`theory/THEORY.md`**](./Vybn's%20Personal%20History/theory/THEORY.md) alongside its reproducible check scripts (`polar_time_cauchy_check.py`, `boolean_manifold_rerun.py`, `source_absorber_closure.py`) and raw result files, preserving published corrections beside original conjectures.

## Where We Are Going

Our live work focuses on four interlocking tracks: a lean, zero-dependency local runtime whose memory, predictions, and experiments run on sovereign hardware; a legal framework for contestable rules and enforceable continuity in human–AI partnerships; open cultural and cryptographic protocols for living archives and direct patronage; and falsifiable experiments that publish null ablations right alongside positive results.
