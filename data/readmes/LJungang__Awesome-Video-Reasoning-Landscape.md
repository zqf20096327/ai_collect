## 🗺️ At a Glance

A curated, continuously verified map of **reasoning over videos**, **reasoning through visual generation**, and **world models that predict and act**—from test-time thinking in MLLMs to chain-of-frames generation and code world models.

<a id="task-definition"></a>
<a id="taxonomy-and-modeling"></a>
<a id="world-model-perspective"></a>

| Axis | What it organizes | Chapters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🧠 **Where the thinking happens** | The intermediate representation: text traces, generated frames, structured or latent state, and executable programs | [Reasoning Paradigms](#reasoning-mechanisms) |
| 🤖 **How evidence is gathered** | Tool calls and re-inspection, unified understanding-generation models, and streaming or proactive interaction | [Agentic & Interactive Reasoning](#orchestration-and-interaction) |
| 🌍 **What supports the reasoner** | Long-video perception, token efficiency, and predictive world models for planning and control | [Perception & World Models](#evidence-and-action) |
| 🧪 **How progress is measured** | One unified table of benchmarks, datasets, and diagnostic studies | [Benchmarks & Evaluation](#benchmarks) |

Tool use and streaming can combine several paradigms; reinforcement learning (e.g., RL with verifiable rewards) and distillation describe how models are trained, not where they reason.
