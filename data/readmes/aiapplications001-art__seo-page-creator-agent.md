## Operational Boundaries

- Google integrations are treated as read-only.
- The standard workflow does not publish to a CMS or update live pages automatically.
- Local publishing generates HTML in a configured Git website repository.
- Commit approval and push or deploy approval are separate steps.
- Batch runs may queue many pages, but each page moves through the V2 workflow one at a time.
- Missing research, unsupported claims, and hard quality-gate failures cannot be bypassed.
- Hidden SEO-only internal links are not allowed.
- Competitor names, third-party logos, screenshots, and external brand visuals require approval before inclusion.

## Batch and LIVE Publishing

### Create a Batch of 100 Pages

Use one planning command to create a batch queue:

```bash
seo-agent batch plan \
  --batch-id saas-pages-001 \
  --company "Example SaaS" \
  --product "Accounting Software" \
  --target-count 100 \
  --market "United States" \
  --cluster accounting-software \
  --strategy "commercial comparison and category expansion"
```

Behind the scenes, the batch workflow can:

1. Store the batch plan, page queue, state, and summary.
2. Recommend the next eligible page.
3. Route that page through the V2 SEO workflow one page at a time.
4. Record research, quality gates, approvals, repairs, failures, and acceptance.
5. Continue to the next eligible page without losing the state of blocked or failed pages.
6. Generate a batch summary for editors, SEO leads, agency teams, and brand stakeholders.

### Generate LIVE HTML Pages Locally

Approved batches can be connected to a Git-backed website repository:

```bash
seo-agent batch publish-target save \
  --target-id website-main \
  --repo-path /path/to/website \
  --branch main \
  --html-root public/seo \
  --url-base https://example.com/seo

seo-agent batch publish-plan --batch-id saas-pages-001 --target-id website-main
seo-agent batch publish-commit --batch-id saas-pages-001 --target-id website-main --approved-by "Editor Name"
seo-agent batch publish-push --batch-id saas-pages-001 --target-id website-main --approved-by "Editor Name"
```

Publishing remains approval-gated. The workflow does not silently push or deploy generated pages.

### Batch State and Uniqueness

Batch runs persist state under:

```text
.seo-agent-workspace/batch-runs/<run-id>/
  batch-run.json
  run-ledger.jsonl
  current-page.lock
```

Content and structure uniqueness are required across the batch and across historical runs. A page must fail repair if it reuses one shared HTML body template, repeats a same body section pattern, or differs only by title, slug, hook, or paraphrase.

## Inside SEO Page Creator Agent: How the AI SEO Workflow Works

![Animated SEO Page Creator Agent workflow](docs/images/seo-page-creator-workflow.svg)

The workflow moves from website context and keyword research to a LIVE web page in thirteen connected steps:

1. **Step 1 - Website onboarding:** Capture business, audience, market, brand, and conversion context.
2. **Step 2 - Site intelligence:** Read the sitemap, existing pages, metadata, and content coverage.
3. **Step 3 - Keyword research:** Identify demand, query groups, clusters, and business-fit opportunities.
4. **Step 4 - Page intent:** Choose a learning, evaluation, or action-oriented page format.
5. **Step 5 - SERP research:** Review top results, competitors, People Also Ask patterns, and search features.
6. **Step 6 - Audience research:** Study YouTube, Reddit, forums, reviews, and customer language.
7. **Step 7 - Unique angle:** Identify information gain, content gaps, and differentiation from competitors.
8. **Step 8 - SEO content brief:** Define depth, sources, voice, links, and content requirements.
9. **Step 9 - SEO page outline:** Map page flow, sections, examples, and reader journey.
10. **Step 10 - First draft:** Create human-readable content with claims and asset requirements.
11. **Step 11 - On-page SEO optimization:** Improve headings, answer placement, keywords, metadata, links, and schema opportunities.
12. **Step 12 - QA, citations, and AI visibility:** Check evidence, claims, citations, readability, page quality, and AI-searchability.
13. **Step 13 - LIVE web page and publishing:** Generate the page preview and move to approved publishing steps.

## Workflow Gates

SEO Page Creator Agent does not treat a first draft as a finished SEO page. Each page moves through research, strategy, human-like language, quality, and publishing-readiness checks.

These gates help founders, editors, SEO teams, agencies, and freelance SEO consultants understand what is ready, what needs improvement, and what evidence is still missing.

### Five Mandatory Research and Content Gates

1. **SERP Research Ledger Gate:** Confirms that the page is based on meaningful search results, competitor pages, SERP features, search intent, and source evidence.
2. **Social and Video Research Gate:** Captures real audience language, questions, objections, and experiences from YouTube, Reddit, forums, reviews, and support conversations.
3. **Audience Definition Gate:** Identifies who the page is for, what they need, what problem they are solving, and what decision they may make next.
4. **Narrative Brief Gate:** Defines the page's primary promise, unique angle, structure, differentiation factor, examples, and recommended call to action.
5. **Citation Set Gate:** Connects important claims to appropriate sources, records evidence and source notes, and identifies unsupported or risky statements.

### Human-Like Language and Quality Gates

After the mandatory research gates, the page moves through additional checks:

- **Human-Like Language Check:** Confirms that the page uses natural, readable language, reflects the brand voice, avoids repetitive AI writing patterns, and feels relevant to the intended audience and market.
- **Structured QA Gate:** Checks headings, answer placement, readability, keyword coverage, internal links, citations, claims, schema opportunities, and AI-searchability.
- **Content-Ready Gate:** Marks the page ready for editorial review only after the required gates and structured QA checks pass.
- **Image-Readiness Gate:** Confirms that image slots have approved assets, valid asset locations, or accepted prompt-based fallbacks.
- **Publish-Ready Gate:** Confirms that the content and image requirements are complete before an approved local publishing workflow begins.

### What Happens When a Gate Fails?

The agent records the missing evidence, failed check, content risk, or required repair. It does not silently treat an incomplete page as ready.

Hard failures such as missing research or unsupported claims must be resolved before the page can move forward. Advisory recommendations can be reviewed and overridden when an editor has a clear reason.

## Advanced Quality Contracts

SEO Page Creator Agent uses structured quality contracts to keep research, writing, citations, QA, images, and page readiness connected.

These contracts help ensure that a page is not treated as complete simply because an AI agent produced readable text.

### Research Contracts

Each page can maintain structured artifacts for:

- SERP research and competitor analysis.
- Social, video, Reddit, forum, and customer-language research.
- Audience definition and search-intent context.
- Narrative brief, page angle, differentiation, and decision guidance.
- Citation sets, evidence, source notes, and claim mapping.

### Content Contracts

The workflow also tracks:

- Page type, primary keyword, secondary keywords, and content cluster.
- Required sections, claims, examples, transitions, and internal links.
- Human-like language, brand voice, readability, and non-generic writing patterns.
- Page refresh scope, changed sections, affected claims, and update rationale.
- Section-level version history and editorial decisions.

### QA and Readiness Contracts

Before a page can advance, the workflow checks:

- Required artifacts and schema versions.
- Machine validation results.
- Host-agent judgment checks.
- Blocking issues and advisory recommendations.
- Editorial QA JSON and readable QA reports.
- Image slots, asset locations, licensing approval, and prompt fallbacks.
- Content-ready and publish-ready state requirements.

### How Enforcement Works

1. **Machine checks** validate structure, required fields, schemas, counts, links, and artifact relationships.
2. **Judgment checks** evaluate research quality, audience fit, narrative strength, evidence, and content usefulness.
3. **Blocking issues** must be fixed before the page can move forward.
4. **Advisory recommendations** can be reviewed or overridden with editorial reasoning.
5. **Missing research, unsupported claims, and required artifacts cannot be bypassed.**

A page can be marked `content_ready` only after the mandatory research gates, human-like language checks, expanded page packet, and structured QA report pass.

A page can be marked `publish_ready` only after the content is ready and required image assets or approved fallbacks are complete.

### Intent-Aware Page Structure

SEO Page Creator Agent includes an Intent-Aware Page Structure contract. Page sections must carry page-specific `sectionIntent` and `evidenceBudget` expectations instead of relying on one fixed body template.

The `pre-draft-quality-brief.json` artifact checks publish-worthiness before prose is written. It includes the SERP Superiority Gate and the Research-Derived Structure Gate. The research-derived structure plan is stored as `researchDerivedStructurePlan`, and final copy must include `structurePlanDeliveryProof`.

For competitive pages, the SERP Superiority Gate expects primary keyword top 5 SERP pages, a secondary keyword or long-tail top 3 SERP set, the top 4 intent dimensions, at least 1 required superiority component, and 5 differentiated visible improvements. Final prose must include `superiorityProof`, `whyThisDeservesToRank`, and traceable `sourceRefs`.

Opportunity selection prioritizes unfocused and long-tail search opportunities where the page can add real information gain. The agent should use SERP, PAA, Reddit/forum/video, AI Overview, audience-language, and competitor-gap research to avoid generic page structures and shallow copy.
