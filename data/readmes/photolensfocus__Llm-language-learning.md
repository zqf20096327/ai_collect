## Why Traditional Learning Methods Always Fail You

In the past, anyone trying to master spoken English had to choose between two main paths, both burdened with massive drawbacks:

### 1. Hiring Private Human Tutors (Expensive + Scheduling Hassles)

* **High Costs:** A quality native-speaking tutor can easily cost dozens or hundreds of dollars per session. Keeping up a daily practice routine is simply unsustainable for most budgets.

### 2. Finding Language Partners or Talking to the Wall (Social Anxiety + Lack of Feedback)

* **Social Anxiety:** Speaking with a real person often triggers the fear of making grammar mistakes, sounding awkward, or being judged for your accent.
* **No Real-Time Feedback:** Talking to yourself in the mirror doesn't provide corrections. You end up repeating low-level mistakes without making real progress.
