## 走进故事 / Step into a story

选一本故事，扮演主角。选择下一步，或写下自己想做的事，AI 会接着写下你的故事。你也可以创作自己的故事，邀请他人来读。

Choose a story and step into the lead role. Pick your next move or write your own action, and AI continues the story. You can also create a story of your own and invite others to read it.

- **发现与阅读 / Discover and read** — 探索不同题材，在手机或电脑浏览器中开始阅读。 / Explore stories across genres in your phone or desktop browser.
- **参与故事 / Take part** — 选择行动，或用自己的话决定接下来做什么。 / Choose an action or write what you want to do next.
- **创作故事 / Create** — 设定人物与开篇，写下属于自己的互动故事。 / Set up characters and an opening for an interactive story of your own.
- **随时续读 / Continue** — 保存故事进度，从书架接着读。 / Save your progress and return to your story from your bookshelf.

无需注册即可开始，每日提供免费体力。界面支持中文、English 和日本語。

Start without signing up, with free daily Energy. The interface is available in Chinese, English, and Japanese.
