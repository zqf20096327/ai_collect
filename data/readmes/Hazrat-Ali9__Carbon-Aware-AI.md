# 🌱🤖 Carbon-Aware AI — Building Sustainable & Energy-Efficient Artificial Intelligence

**Carbon-Aware-AI** is an advanced research project focused on building **environmentally responsible, energy-efficient, and carbon-conscious Artificial Intelligence systems**.

As AI models become increasingly computationally intensive, their energy consumption and associated carbon footprint have become important sustainability challenges. This project explores how **carbon intensity, energy consumption, model efficiency, hardware utilization, and intelligent scheduling** can be incorporated into the AI development and deployment lifecycle.

The goal is to move beyond simply building more powerful AI toward building **smarter, greener, and more sustainable AI systems**. Recent research highlights approaches such as model compression, quantization, pruning, efficient hardware, carbon-aware scheduling, and lifecycle-aware measurement as important directions for sustainable AI.

## ✨ Core Research Areas

* 🌱 **Carbon-Aware Machine Learning**

  * Incorporate carbon footprint into ML optimization
  * Balance model performance with environmental impact
  * Track energy and CO₂e-related metrics

* ⚡ **Energy-Efficient AI**

  * Reduce computational requirements
  * Explore model compression and pruning
  * Investigate efficient inference strategies

* 🌍 **Carbon-Aware Scheduling**

  * Schedule computational workloads based on grid carbon intensity
  * Explore renewable-energy-aware training
  * Optimize when and where AI workloads execute

* 🧠 **Green Model Optimization**

  * Quantization
  * Knowledge distillation
  * Sparsity
  * Lightweight architectures
  * Efficient neural networks

* 🖥️ **Hardware-Aware AI**

  * Analyze GPU/CPU energy consumption
  * Explore accelerator efficiency
  * Study hardware–software co-design

* 📊 **AI Carbon Footprint Tracking**

  * Estimate training emissions
  * Measure inference energy
  * Compare carbon impact across models and configurations
