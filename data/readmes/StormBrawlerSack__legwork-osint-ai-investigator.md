## Why this exists

Every investigation starts the same way: twenty tabs, a notepad, and a question that keeps growing arms. The tabs win, the question loses.

## Why it is different

Search engines answer queries; Legwork answers questions. It reads the sources it finds and writes the answer with a citation on every sentence — so you can check it, not just trust it.


## The idea

- **One object.** A highlighter: the phrase a source backs up gets marked, and the source sits in the margin next to it.
- **One number.** How many public pages it read to write the answer.
- **Colour.** Ink `#121114` and paper `#F4F1EC`. Highlighter pink `#FF85C0` marks what is sourced, and the download.
- **The main feature.** No source, no sentence: every line of an answer carries the public pages it came from, single-source claims are left out, and gaps are stated instead of guessed.

## The demo on the page

The question types itself in, the page count runs up, and the answer is written one line at a time: the key phrase gets the highlighter, each sentence carries numbered source chips, and the sources line up as cards under the answer. The line that actually answers the question is held back, and the card offers the download instead. Three sample questions can be switched under the answer.
