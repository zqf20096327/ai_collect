'AI Cadence' is an open-source initiative that explores significant developments across the Artificial Intelligence ecosystem.

From foundation models and AI tools to agent frameworks, infrastructure, research, and emerging innovations, 'AI Cadence' aims to help developers, architects, researchers, and technology enthusiasts stay informed about what's happening in AI.

The goal is to go beyond news aggregation by providing concise, meaningful updates that explain **what changed, why it matters, and where to learn more.**

## Our Philosophy

### Know What You Don't Know.

Artificial Intelligence is evolving at an unprecedented pace. New models, tools, frameworks, research, and innovations emerge continuously. Keeping up with everything is practically impossible.

But there is a deeper challenge: sometimes, we don't even know what we are missing.

The biggest knowledge gaps are not always the things we know we don't understand, but the things we don't yet realize we should understand.

'AI Cadence' is built around this idea.

Through hand-curated developments across the AI ecosystem, the aim is to broaden awareness, uncover emerging ideas, and help people discover technologies and concepts they might otherwise miss.

It is not about consuming more information. It is about discovering what matters, expanding our understanding, questioning what we already know, and continuously learning.

**Because knowing what you don't know is the beginning of meaningful learning.**

## Editorial Principles

'AI Cadence' aims to maintain a high standard of relevance, accuracy, and usefulness.

- **Curated, not exhaustive:** Focus on meaningful developments rather than publishing every AI announcement.
- **Source-driven:** Prefer official announcements, documentation, research papers, and original repositories.
- **Context matters:** Explain the significance and practical implications of important developments.
- **Concise and accessible:** Present information in a format that is easy to scan and understand.
- **Technically grounded:** Distinguish actual capabilities from marketing claims and speculation.
- **Transparent:** Clearly distinguish released features, previews, experiments, and announcements.
- **Discovery-oriented:** Look beyond popular announcements to uncover lesser-known tools, research, and emerging ideas.
