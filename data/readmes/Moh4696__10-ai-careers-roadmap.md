## 🧭 How to use this library

1. **Pick one path.** Don't try to learn all ten. Choose the one that excites you most.
2. **Open its page** and read the `🎯 Start here` block — it's a short ordered path, not a dump of links.
3. **Go deep, then wide.** Finish one course before collecting five more. Momentum beats bookmarks.
4. **Build in public.** Ship small projects to GitHub as you go — that portfolio *is* your resume.
5. **Join a community** (linked on each page). Learning with people is 10x faster than learning alone.
