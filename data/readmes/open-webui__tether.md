# Tether

![Tether](./demo.png)

Your [Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui), wherever you work.

Tether extends Open WebUI beyond the browser. Interact with your AI right from your desktop, and give it the access to work directly on your computer.

Ironically, the app called Tether untethers you from your desk. [We can explain.](MANIFESTO.md)

### Workspace sandbox

**Room to work. Boundaries you choose.**

On macOS and supported Windows devices, restrict Open Terminal to a local folder
in **Settings → Open Terminal → Sandbox**. Workspace sandboxing is unavailable on Linux.
See the [sandbox guide](docs/sandbox.md) for platform support, behavior, and limitations.
