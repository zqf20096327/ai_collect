# ai-ml-roadmap
# AI/ML Roadmap

A structured, self-paced roadmap designed to help beginners navigate the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning using high-quality, free resources.

---

## Live Demo

https://dakshv-star.github.io/ai-ml-roadmap/

---

## Overview

Learning AI/ML can feel fragmented and overwhelming. Resources are abundant, but direction is not.

This project brings clarity by organizing the learning process into a logical, step-by-step path. It is built for individuals who want a focused approach without relying on expensive programs.

---

## Key Features

* Structured progression from fundamentals to advanced topics
* Carefully selected free learning resources
* Clean, distraction-free interface
* Built-in progress tracking for consistency
* Designed for self-paced learning
* XP, Streak and Level system

---

## Who This Is For

* Students beginning their journey in AI/ML
* Individuals transitioning into technical fields
* Self-learners looking for a clear and reliable roadmap

---

## Philosophy

This roadmap is based on a simple idea:

> Direction matters more than volume.

Instead of overwhelming learners with countless links, it emphasizes:

* What to learn
* When to learn it
* Why it matters
