# coregold-usdt-vcc-apple-wallet-face-id-pay-guide

**Mastering Hardware-Encrypted Checkouts: How to Link CoreGold Virtual Cards to Apple Pay**

Manually typing virtual card numbers into web forms often triggers secondary verification friction or checkout blocks. The most reliable method to clear cross-border SaaS checkouts is tokenizing your virtual card directly into **Apple Pay**.

This tutorial demonstrates how to mount a CoreGold VCC into your US Apple Wallet and retrieve real-time OTP verification codes directly from your dashboard.

---

## Step 1: Prepare Your Apple Ecosystem

1. Ensure your iPhone or Mac is logged into a supported Apple ID (US-region accounts offer highest compatibility).
2. Open the native **Apple Wallet** app.
3. Tap the **`+`** icon and select **Add Payment Card**.

---

## Step 2: Input CoreGold VCC Credentials

1. Choose **Enter Card Details Manually** (do not use camera scan).
2. Retrieve your 16-digit card number, expiration date, and CVV from your [CoreGold Dashboard](https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b) under **Card Management**.
3. Input the credentials into Apple Wallet.

---

## Step 3: Retrieve Verification Codes Instantly inside CoreGold

1. When Apple Wallet prompts for card verification, select **Email Verification**.
2. Instead of checking your external inbox, return to your CoreGold card details panel.
3. Click the **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** button at the bottom of the page to view the live verification code instantly.
4. Input the code back into Apple Wallet to finalize tokenization.

---

## Step 4: One-Tap Face ID Checkouts

Once linked, your CoreGold VCC acts as a native local debit card. Double-click your device power button and authenticate via Face ID to pay for Claude Pro, Notion, or App Store purchases instantly.
