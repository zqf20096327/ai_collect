### 1. Why 3D & Interactive AI Studios Break Standard Payment Rails

Commercial spatial and game-asset production exposes three core payment vulnerabilities:

- **High-Ticket Studio Tier Rejections**: Commercial 3D and world-model subscriptions carry higher monthly price points than basic text tools, triggering elevated fraud scoring on non-US consumer cards paired with cross-border proxy sessions.
- **Multi-Seat Card Fingerprint Linkage**: Studios provisioning 5–15 seats across concept artists and technical artists on one physical credit card risk shared card-fingerprint flags and retroactive account freezes.
- **Project-Cycle Renewal Leakage**: Game production runs in intense pre-production and vertical-slice sprints. Leaving high-tier subscriptions attached to non-disposable cards leads to forgotten recurring charges after a milestone wraps.
