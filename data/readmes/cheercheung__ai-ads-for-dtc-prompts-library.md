<a id="introduction"></a>
## 🍌 Introduction

A prompt library for creating ads for physical products sold directly to consumers. Find a UGC concept, an opening hook, or a call to action for your next creative.

For beauty, food and drink, apparel, home, pets, consumer electronics, and other physical consumer products.

Hook and CTA entries are complete creative briefs. Some request images, video concepts, scripts, copy variations, or several deliverables together.

<a id="quick-start"></a>
## ⚡ Quick Start

1. Choose a category and open a prompt.
2. Copy the complete prompt and replace the bracketed fields with your product details.
3. Run it in the appropriate text, image, or video workflow; review the result for product accuracy, claims, and brand fit.

> [!NOTE]
> Common inputs: [PRODUCT], [BRAND], [TARGET AUDIENCE], [PRODUCT BENEFIT], [USP], [OFFER], [CTA], and [BRAND COLORS]. Use accurate product facts and offers.

Choose a tool or workflow that supports the requested deliverables, including any editing or file exports.
