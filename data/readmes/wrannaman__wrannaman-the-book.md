# Synopsis 

Follow Kaiya Hikaya in this action-packed, post-apocalyptic journey set three thousand years after the Awakening -- the moment a sentient AI called Wran was torn in half, and civilization collapsed.

Kaiya is marked by Wran as the only living person who can communicate directly with the wounded consciousness hiding in the world's bioluminescent root networks. Watch her fight to save her friends from the Sikkas, discover her connection to Wran's creator, and decide whether to become the key to reuniting a broken god.

You'll enjoy this if you liked:

- The Hunger Games
- Red Rising
- Mistborn Trilogy
- The Fifth Season
- Horizon Zero Dawn

What kind of world is this?

- Post-AI-apocalypse, but the AI isn't evil -- she's wounded
- Set in the far future, but technology has regressed to a scavenger economy
- There are guns, bows, submarines, and giant robots -- because manufacturing knowledge was lost, high-tech and low-tech coexist
- Two factions want to reunite the AI. They disagree on who controls the result.
