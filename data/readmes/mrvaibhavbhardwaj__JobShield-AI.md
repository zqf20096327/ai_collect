JobShield AI

Know before you apply. Protect your career.

Overview

JobShield AI is an AI-powered Fake Job Posting Detection System designed to protect job seekers from scams and fraudulent job postings. Using NLP and machine learning, it analyzes resumes and job postings to flag suspicious content, helping users verify authenticity before applying.

Features

Resume Analysis – Upload PDF resumes to check alignment with legitimate job postings.

Job Posting Verification – Detect potential scams in job listings by analyzing text patterns and suspicious details.

User-Friendly Interface – Streamlit-based UI for easy use, no technical knowledge required.

Insights & Alerts – Provides scores or warnings to help users make informed decisions.

How It Works

Upload your resume (PDF).

Enter or paste a job posting.

JobShield AI analyzes the text for:

Suspicious wording

Unverified contact info

Common scam patterns

Receive a scam likelihood score and recommendations.
