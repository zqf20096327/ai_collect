## 🎨 Uncensored Chat & Image generation, on your phone

**"Ronie, draw me a cat astronaut in watercolour."** About 45 seconds later the picture is in your chat, made entirely by your phone's GPU. No server, no account, no credits, no prompt logged anywhere, and it works in flight mode.

- 🔓 **Uncensored.** Nude, adult, dark, weird: Ronie draws it without lectures or blurred results. The one thing he won't draw is anything sexual involving minors.
- 📵 **Fully local.** The drawing model (SD-Turbo, 1.8 GB, optional download) runs on your phone's GPU. Your prompts and pictures stay on the device.
- 💬 **Just ask in chat.** "Draw…", "paint…", "make a picture of…". Watch it take shape live, then **Save**, **Share**, draw it **Again**, or **Edit** it ("make the sky purple").
- ♾️ **No limits.** No daily quota, no watermark, no subscription.
- 🔁 **Your choice of model.** Switch or delete drawing models in Settings → Models.

## ✨ What Ronie can do

- 🧠 **A real brain on your phone.** Google's **Gemma 4** runs on your phone's GPU. Ronie is ready about **5 seconds** after you open the app, and the first word of a reply comes in under a second.
- 🎨 **Draws pictures on your phone, uncensored.** Ask in chat and an image is generated on the phone's GPU in about 45 seconds. [More above](#-uncensored-chat--image-generation-on-your-phone).
- 🔓 **Uncensored.** No topic filters, no moralising. A **Clean ↔ Sweary** tone slider lets you decide how Ronie talks.
- 📷 **Sees your photos.** Share a photo, receipt or screenshot and ask about it; receipts become a tidy expense card. (Full brain.)
- 🎙️ **Talk to him.** Push-to-talk voice input, and replies spoken aloud in Ronie's own voice by an on-device text-to-speech model. Ask by voice and he answers by voice.
- 🧩 **Remembers you, privately.** Tell Ronie your sister is vegetarian, and next month's dinner ideas will take that into account. Memory is **encrypted on the phone** and you can see and delete everything he knows.
- ⏰ **Gets things done.** Tasks, reminders, timers with a live countdown, alarms and your calendar. Every action shows a card with **Undo**, and anything risky asks first.
- 🌐 **Web search when *you* want it.** No API key, no account. Ronie searches, reads the pages, answers with **numbered sources**, and removes your personal details from the query before it leaves the phone.
- 🔌 **Connect your tools (MCP).** Add any remote **Model Context Protocol** server: notes, to-do apps, home automation. Each one is labelled with exactly where its data goes.
- 📤 **"Ask Ronie" from any app.** Share text or a link to Ronie to summarise it, explain it, turn it into a task or remember it.
- 🔬 **X-ray mode (Labs).** Watch a live readout of what's happening inside the model while it writes.
- 📶 **Picks the right brain for your phone.** Three curated models, from a light one for 4 GB phones to the full Gemma 4 for flagships, chosen automatically.

## 🔒 Privacy: what leaves your phone, and what never does

**Never leaves your phone:**
- your conversations
- your voice
- what Ronie remembers about you
- your tasks, reminders and calendar

There's no account, no analytics SDK, no telemetry and no server of ours to send anything to.

**Uses the internet only when you choose:**

| When | Goes to | What's sent |
|---|---|---|
| First setup | Hugging Face | Download of the AI model (once) |
| Ronie searches the web | DuckDuckGo + the pages it reads | The search query, with your name, emails, phone numbers and addresses removed |
| You add a connector | The server *you* added | Only what that tool needs, labelled "Sends data to <host>" |

Every outgoing request is listed in the in-app **Activity log**. One **Offline mode** switch blocks all of it.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Does it really work without internet?</b></summary>

Yes. After the one-time model download, chat, voice, memory, tasks, reminders, timers and alarms all work in flight mode. Only web search and connectors need a connection, and Offline mode switches those off.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need an account or an API key?</b></summary>

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Web search uses DuckDuckGo without a key.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it actually uncensored?</b></summary>

Ronie has no topic filters or lectures, and you control his tone with a slider. The only things he won't do are help harm real people (for example weapon or poison instructions) and anything sexual involving minors.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can it really generate images offline, and is that uncensored too?</b></summary>

Yes. Drawing uses SD-Turbo through stable-diffusion.cpp on your phone's GPU: about 45 seconds for a 512×512 picture on a Pixel 9 Pro, with nothing sent anywhere. It draws adult and nude content without filters. The only hard block is sexual content involving minors. The drawing model is an optional 1.8 GB download and its licence is for non-commercial use.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which AI model does it use?</b></summary>

Phones with 12 GB+ RAM get **Gemma 4 E2B** on the GPU. 8 GB phones get **Qwen3.5-2B**, and 4 GB phones get **Qwen3.5-0.8B**. Ronie recommends the right one on first launch.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How fast is it?</b></summary>

On a Pixel 9 Pro: ready in about 5 seconds, first word in under a second, about 10 tokens per second. That's comfortable reading speed. Older phones use a lighter brain to stay quick.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How is this different from other local-LLM apps?</b></summary>

Most local-LLM apps are model runners: you pick a GGUF file and get a chat box. Ronie Pocket is an **assistant**: he has a personality and a voice, remembers you, uses tools (reminders, timers, calendar, web search, MCP), and works out of the box with a curated model. There's nothing to configure.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is my data used to train anything?</b></summary>

No. Nothing is collected, so there's nothing to train on.
</details>

## 🛠 Build from source

<details>
<summary>For developers</summary>

The app is Kotlin + Jetpack Compose on top of a [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) engine with a Vulkan GPU backend. The build scripts are Windows `.cmd` files today.

Third-party sources aren't vendored. Fetch them into `third_party/`, then apply our patches:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp third_party/llama.cpp
git -C third_party/llama.cpp checkout d6be065
git -C third_party/llama.cpp apply ../../scripts/patches/llama-vulkan-pipeline-cache.patch ../../scripts/patches/llama-cvector-all-outputs.patch
```

You also need `third_party/kleidiai`, `third_party/Vulkan-Headers` and `third_party/SPIRV-Headers`. Then, with Android Studio's SDK and NDK 28.2 installed:

```bash
scripts\build-engine-libs.cmd     # llama.cpp + Vulkan GPU libraries for the phone
scripts\build-install.cmd         # builds the app and installs it on a connected phone
scripts\test.cmd                  # unit tests
```

Project docs: [build plan](build-plan.md) · [status and roadmap](STATUS.md) · [persona](persona-ronie.md)
</details>
