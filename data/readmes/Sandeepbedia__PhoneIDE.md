# 🚀 PhoneIDE

**Code • Build • Debug • Create Anywhere.**

PhoneIDE is a mobile development environment designed to bring powerful coding, project management, AI assistance and application development tools directly to Android devices.

---

## 📱 Project Information

- **Application:** PhoneIDE
- **Package Name:** `com.io.phone.ide`
- **Latest Reported Version:** `2.8.2-V7-Parvaaz`
- **Version Code:** 420 (v2.8.0)
- **Minimum SDK:** 24
- **Target SDK:** 35 (reported in v2.6.9)
- **Platform:** Android
- **Architecture:** 32-bit & 64-bit
