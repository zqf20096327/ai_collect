## Practical Knowledge: Why Mobile Wallets Beat Manual Web Forms

### 1. How Device Wallet Tokenization Reduces Checkout Friction
When you pay directly on a web form by typing a 16-digit card number, payment gateways inspect browser telemetry, IP location, and form-entry signals aggressively. By contrast, when you bind a card into **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**, your device's secure hardware generates a tokenized credential (DPAN) paired with biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID / device unlock). Payment processors treat this as a verified device-present transaction, reducing unnecessary verification loops on supported checkouts.

### 2. Universal Digital Wallet Support on CoreGold
All CoreGold card BINs can be bound to **Apple Pay** and **Google Pay** (usable wherever the card, merchant, and device support digital wallets), as well as scenarios covering **Google Play / Apple Store**, AI tools, and overseas e-commerce, ads, and cloud services.

### 3. Understanding the Two-Step Email OTP Flow
Many first-time users get stuck waiting for a verification code in their personal email inbox when adding a card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. Here is how it actually works:
1. In **Apple Wallet** or **Google Wallet**, enter your CoreGold card number, expiration date, and CVV manually and select **email verification**.
2. Immediately switch back to your CoreGold dashboard under **Card Management (卡片管理)**, open that card's details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom—the live verification code appears right on screen for you to copy back into your wallet app.

### 4. Matching Your Billing Address Record
Whether you check **Auto-Generate Billing Info** during card issuance or manually input Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code, keep your checkout billing address consistent with your registered card info.

> **Card Tip**: Before purchasing a paid app or subscription on Apple Store or Google Play, add your CoreGold card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet first and verify it—once bound, in-app purchases and subscriptions clear with a single biometric tap.

### Step 1: Register on CoreGold

### Step 2: Deposit USDT via TRON (TRC20)
1. In the left menu, click **My Wallet (我的钱包)** -> **Deposit (充值)**.
2. Enter your deposit amount to display the USDT deposit address and QR code.
3. Open your crypto wallet or exchange (such as OKX or Binance; if you don't hold USDT yet, you can purchase it there first), select USDT withdrawal/transfer, and choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
4. Copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, verify the address, network, and amount, and confirm the transfer. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact customer support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### Step 3: Select Plan, Card Category & Billing Info
1. Click **Apply for Card (申请开卡)** in the left menu and choose an **Individual Plan** (such as Diamond Plan or AI Subscription) or **Team Plan** (check live page details for current pricing, supported scenarios, and fees).
2. Select the matching card category (CoreGold provides US-issued card BINs) and enter the initial deposit amount.
3. Keep **Auto-Generate Billing Info** checked, or uncheck it to enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keeping billing addresses consistent during merchant checkout.

### Step 4: View Card Details & Bind Apple Pay / Google Pay
1. Open **Card Management (卡片管理)** and select your card to view card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, or use top-up, withdrawal, and freeze controls.
2. To bind **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**:
   - Open **Apple Wallet** on iPhone or **Google Wallet** on Android, select **Add Payment Card**, and enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually.
   - Select **email verification** when prompted.
   - Return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code.
   - Enter the verification code back into your mobile wallet to complete binding.
