## 🧭 How to Find More Winners

Most platforms follow a predictable URL pattern. Swap in the event name and you'll land straight on the winners.

| Platform | Pattern | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| **Devpost** | `https://<event>.devpost.com/project-gallery` | Filter by **Winners**; the `/updates` tab has the announcement |
| **Devfolio** | `https://<event>.devfolio.co/projects` | Filter by prize ([guide](https://guide.devfolio.co/docs/guide/Projects/view-submissions)) |
| **ETHGlobal** | `https://ethglobal.com/events/<event>/prizes` | Finalists; browse all projects at `ethglobal.com/showcase` |
| **lablab.ai** | `https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/<event>/live` | Winners and runners-up at the top |
| **Kaggle** | `https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/<competition>/writeups` | Full technical write-ups |
| **AI Tinkerers** | `https://<city>.aitinkerers.org/hackathons/<id>/results` | Use `/all_entries` to see everything |
| **DEV Community** | [`dev.to/devteam`](https://dev.to/devteam) | Winner posts are tagged `#devchallenge` |
| **Hashnode** | [`townhall.hashnode.com`](https://townhall.hashnode.com) | Each winner links to the builder's own write-up |
