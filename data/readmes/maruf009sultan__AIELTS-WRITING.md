## Why I Created This Project

Preparing for the IELTS Writing exam can be frustrating, especially without instant, insightful feedback. Existing free online tools are often basic, while private tutoring is costly. **AIELTS-WRITING** was created to bridge this gap, offering anyone the ability to submit writing and receive near-instant, AI-powered analysis modeled after IELTS scoring criteria.

I wanted to empower self-learners, students, teachers, and anyone aiming to improve their writing skills with:

- **Instant scores and error analysis**
- **Comprehensive, actionable feedback**
- **Personalized AI suggestions and improvements**
- **Simulated examiner evaluation for IELTS and beyond**

---

## 🚀 Features & How It Works

### 1. **Submit Your Writing**

- Submit essays, paragraphs, stories—anything! Just paste your content and hit "Analyze Writing".
- Accepts all forms of writing, not just IELTS essays.

### 2. **AI-Powered Analysis**

- **Detailed Scoring:** Breaks down your writing with band scores for:
  - Task Achievement
  - Coherence & Cohesion
  - Lexical Resource
  - Grammatical Range & Accuracy
- **Overall Band Score:** Emulates IELTS grading for familiar feedback.

### 3. **Granular Error Detection**

- Identifies and highlights grammar, vocabulary, and sentence structure mistakes.
- Offers specific corrections at both word and sentence level.

### 4. **Actionable Feedback and Motivation**

- Gives you feedback, improvement suggestions, and motivational statements to support your learning.
- AI-generated improved writing versions for inspiration.

### 5. **Instant & Secure**

- No submissions are stored on any server by default (API settings are local to your browser).
- Results in seconds.

### 6. **Flexible AI Model Configuration**

- Works with your own OpenAI API Key or compatible endpoint.
- Switch between models (e.g., GPT-4o, GPT-3.5-turbo) or custom endpoints for cost or performance.

## 🤔 FAQ

**Q: Do I need an OpenAI API key?**  
A: You can use your own API key (kept local, not uploaded), or point the app to other OpenAI-compatible endpoints. No API keys are stored server-side.

**Q: What types of writing can I analyze?**  
A: Any English text! While designed for IELTS, the app can provide feedback on stories, emails, business writing, academic assignments, etc.

**Q: How accurate is the feedback?**  
A: The AI models are tuned for IELTS but offer robust general feedback. Band scoring closely mirrors IELTS standards; still, use this as a helpful supplement, not a formal certified evaluation.

**Q: Is my writing data safe?**  
A: Your configuration and results are stored locally in your browser. The default API is OpenAI, which follows their privacy policies. You’re free to use your own endpoint.

**Q: Can I customize the AI model?**  
A: Yes! Pick from GPT-4o, 3.5-turbo, or add a custom model for experimentation or lower costs.
