## ⚡ Key Breakthroughs in GPT Image 2

This collection focuses on the model's four "superpowers":
1. **Flawless Typography:** No more "AI gibberish." Render complex text in English, Cyrillic, and Asian scripts with 100% accuracy.
2. **Identity Retention:** Maintain the same character or visual IP across different scenes and angles.
3. **Structured Design:** Generate complex infographics, exploded views, and multi-panel layouts.
4. **Artistic Intent:** High-fidelity style induction that captures the "soul" of an art movement, not just the surface colors.
