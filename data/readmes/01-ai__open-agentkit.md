# OAK: Open AgentKit

Inspired by OpenAI AgentKit, we believe the community equally needs a truly open and universal "AgentKit"—one that supports all models and ecosystem tools, integrates industry best practices, and adheres to mainstream Agent protocols and standards.

## Why OAK
- **Practical origin, open execution**: OpenAI AgentKit offers useful ideas and components, but it is closed-source and tightly coupled to a single ecosystem, limiting flexibility across many real-world scenarios.
- **Open and universal**: The community needs a vendor-neutral AgentKit that integrates industry best practices (e.g., Claude Skills), supports mainstream Agent protocols and standards, and works across models, tools, and platforms.

## What is OAK
OAK (Open AgentKit) is an open-source initiative to build a general-purpose, ecosystem-agnostic AI Agent platform together with global developers.

Our north star is to be a fully open-source counterpart to OpenAI AgentKit while remaining model- and platform-agnostic.

## Core Modules
- **OAK Framework ([LangCrew](https://github.com/01-ai/langcrew))**: A full-stack multi-Agent development framework enabling efficient architecture and collaboration.
- **OAK Builder**: A community-built visual Agent composer that outputs production-ready code.
- **OAK Runtime**: A stable, reliable runtime for executing and orchestrating Agents at scale.
- **OAK Studio**: A visual workbench for deployment, evaluation, tracing, and continuous optimization.


## Roadmap (High-level)
