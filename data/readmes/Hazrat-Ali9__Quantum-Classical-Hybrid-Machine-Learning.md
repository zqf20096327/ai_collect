# ⚛️🤖 Quantum-Classical Hybrid Machine Learning

**Quantum-Classical-Hybrid-Machine-Learning** is an advanced research project exploring the intersection of **Quantum Computing and Artificial Intelligence** through hybrid quantum-classical machine learning architectures.

The project investigates how **parameterized quantum circuits, quantum feature maps, variational quantum algorithms, and classical neural networks** can work together to create next-generation machine learning systems. The goal is to explore the potential of quantum-enhanced representations while retaining the scalability and optimization capabilities of classical deep learning.

## ✨ Core Research Areas

* ⚛️ **Quantum Machine Learning**

  * Explore quantum-enhanced learning algorithms
  * Investigate quantum feature representations
  * Study the potential of quantum models for ML tasks

* 🧠 **Hybrid Quantum-Classical Architecture**

  * Combine quantum circuits with classical neural networks
  * Optimize quantum and classical parameters jointly
  * Build modular hybrid learning pipelines

* 🔬 **Variational Quantum Circuits**

  * Parameterized quantum gates
  * Trainable quantum ansätze
  * Quantum expectation-value measurements
  * Gradient-based circuit optimization

* 📊 **Quantum Feature Mapping**

  * Encode classical data into quantum states
  * Explore angle and amplitude-based encoding
  * Investigate quantum feature spaces

* 🔄 **Quantum-Classical Optimization**

  * Classical optimizers for quantum parameters
  * Parameter-shift gradient techniques
  * End-to-end hybrid model training

* 📈 **Classical vs Quantum Benchmarking**

  * Compare classical and hybrid architectures
  * Evaluate accuracy, training cost, and scalability
  * Study performance under different circuit configurations
