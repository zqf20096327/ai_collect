## About

Uncensored AI Link is a growing worldwide directory for discovering AI systems that offer configurable moderation, fewer unnecessary refusals, open-weight models, or the ability to run models under your own control.

It covers both **online services** and **local/offline AI**. Not every resource listed here is fully uncensored. Policies, model behaviour, pricing, privacy, and availability can change, so check each provider's current terms.

> **Important:** No directory can truthfully guarantee every AI service in the world. This project combines useful known resources with live discovery indexes so the collection can keep growing.

## 🔒 Privacy, safety, and licensing

- **“Uncensored” is not a formal certification.** A provider may still apply usage rules, moderation, account restrictions, or regional limits.
- **Hosted AI is not the same as local AI.** A cloud service may receive and process your prompts. Read its privacy policy before sharing personal, confidential, or sensitive information.
- **Local does not mean automatically safe.** Download models and extensions from sources you trust; inspect files and permissions.
- **Check model licenses.** Open weights do not always mean unrestricted commercial use or redistribution.
- **Verify every listing.** Links, features, model availability, and policies can change. Report broken or outdated links through an issue.
- Use AI tools lawfully and responsibly. This directory is for legitimate research, development, education, and creative work.

## 📌 Directory maintenance

This is a **curated and continuously expandable directory**, not a claim that every AI service worldwide is listed. Discovery indexes link to much larger, frequently changing catalogues.

When adding a resource, include:
1. Official website or source repository.
2. A short, factual description.
3. Whether it is hosted, local, a model, an API, or a directory.
4. Any known access, license, or platform limitations.
