🧬 AI Autonomous Drug Discovery with Explainability

AI-Autonomous-Drug-Discovery-With-Explainability is a next-generation AI platform that combines autonomous drug discovery, molecular machine learning, explainable AI (XAI), and generative intelligence to accelerate the identification and optimization of therapeutic compounds. The project transforms traditional pharmaceutical research into an interpretable, data-driven, and AI-assisted discovery pipeline by integrating molecular property prediction, candidate generation, toxicity estimation, and transparent decision-making. Modern AI-driven drug discovery increasingly combines predictive modeling, molecular generation, and explainability to improve research efficiency and scientific trust.
