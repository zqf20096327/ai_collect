## Practical Knowledge for Multi-Tool Users

### 1. Choosing Between Individual Plans and Team Plans
CoreGold supports two card-issuance paths under **Apply for Card**:
- **Individual Plans**: On-demand card issuance suited for individual users (such as Diamond Plan or AI Subscription).
- **Team Plans**: Subscription-based packages suited for teams managing multiple cards together.  
*Always check the live page information for current pricing, supported scenarios, and fees.*

### 2. Separating Testing and Ongoing Usage
Using separate cards for different tasks or trials makes it easier to monitor balances and status inside **Card Management**. If a card is no longer needed right now, freezing it or adjusting its balance in **Card Management** helps keep your setup tidy.

### 3. Checking Billing Addresses Before Paying
Whether you rely on **Auto-Generate Billing Info** or enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keep the billing address consistent when paying merchants so checkout details match your card record.

### 4. Smooth Wallet Binding Across Apple Pay & Google Pay
All CoreGold card BINs support binding to **Apple Pay** and **Google Pay** (usable on supported cards, merchants, and devices). Remember the quick flow: enter card details manually in your wallet app -> select email verification -> open your card details page in CoreGold and click **Add to Digital Wallet** at the bottom to get the code -> enter the code back into your wallet.

> **Card Tip**: Even if you don't have USDT on hand right now, you can buy USDT via exchanges like OKX or Binance and transfer it using the **TRON (TRC20)** network once you register your CoreGold account.

## Simple Step-by-Step Setup Tutorial

### Step 1: Register an Account
Go to [https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b](https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b), register using a regular email address, and log in.

### Step 2: Deposit USDT via TRON (TRC20)
1. Click **My Wallet (我的钱包)** -> **Deposit (充值)** in the left menu.
2. Enter your deposit amount to display the USDT deposit address and QR code.
3. Open your crypto wallet or exchange (such as OKX or Binance), select USDT withdrawal/transfer, and choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
4. Double-check the address, network, and amount before confirming. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact customer support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### Step 3: Select Plan, Card Category & Billing Details
1. Click **Apply for Card (申请开卡)** in the left menu and choose an **Individual Plan** or **Team Plan**.
2. Select the matching card category (CoreGold provides US-issued card BINs) and enter the initial deposit amount.
3. Check **Auto-Generate Billing Info** or uncheck it to fill in Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keeping billing addresses consistent during checkout.

### Step 4: View Card & Bind Apple Pay / Google Pay
1. Open **Card Management (卡片管理)** and select your card to view card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, or use top-up, withdrawal, and freeze features.
2. To link **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**:
   - Open **Apple Wallet** or **Google Wallet**, select **Add Payment Card**, and enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually.
   - Choose **email verification** when prompted.
   - Return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code.
   - Enter the verification code back into your mobile wallet to complete binding.
