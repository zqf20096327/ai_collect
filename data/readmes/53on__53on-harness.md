# 53ON Harness

53ON Harness is the Windows app of [53ON](https://53on.com/harness/): it gives your AI a memory on your own PC ([MemoriaBios](https://53on.com/memoriabios/)), and hands, eyes and voice.

## Download

[53on-setup.exe](https://github.com/53on/53on-harness/releases/download/v1.0.101/53on-setup.exe), version 1.0.101, signed. It installs per user, without administrator rights; Windows Settings > Apps removes it.

## Updates

This is the version submitted to winget, under Microsoft's review. 53ON updates itself from 53on.com: when a new version is out it asks you first, and an update whose signature or hash does not match is refused.
