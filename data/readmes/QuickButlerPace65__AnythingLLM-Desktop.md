## 🚀 What is this project?

This is a customized, enhanced fork of the original AnythingLLM Desktop application, specifically optimized and packaged as a **Portable Windows Application**. 

It transforms your desktop into a full-stack AI powerhouse, allowing you to interact with large language models (LLMs) and your own data securely. Because it's completely portable, you can run it from any folder or even a USB flash drive without touching your system registry or running complex installers.

## 🌟 Why this Fork?

While the original AnythingLLM is fantastic, this fork was created to provide a lightweight, no-install experience for Windows users, along with several under-the-hood enhancements.

*(Note: Update these points with your actual specific changes)*
*   🧳 **True Portability:** No installers, no hidden AppData folders. Everything runs straight from the extracted folder.
*   ⚡ **Optimized Performance:** Faster document parsing and smoother UI rendering.
*   🎨 **UI/UX Refinements:** A cleaner, more intuitive interface tailored for power users.
*   🛠️ **Expanded Customization:** Deeper control over system prompts, chunking parameters, and RAG pipelines.

## 🔥 Key Features

*   🏃‍♂️ **Zero Installation:** Download the archive, extract, and double-click `Main.exe`. That's it.
*   🔒 **100% Privacy by Default:** Run local models (via Ollama, LM Studio, etc.) and keep all your documents and chats strictly on your machine.
*   🧠 **Bring Your Own LLM:** Seamlessly switch between Local LLMs, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral, and more.
*   📚 **Enterprise-Grade RAG:** Drop in PDFs, Word docs, TXT files, or web links. The app automatically vectorizes them so you can "chat" with your data.
*   🗂️ **Workspaces:** Isolate context. Create separate workspaces for coding, creative writing, or financial analysis—each with its own documents and system prompts.

## 📦 Quick Start (How to Run)

Since this is a portable version, getting started takes less than a minute.

1. Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/QuickButlerPace65/AnythingLLM-Desktop/releases/tag/1.2.8).
2. Download the latest `.zip` archive.
3. Extract the `.zip` file to any folder on your computer (or a portable drive).
4. Double-click **`Main.exe`** to launch the application.
5. *Enjoy your local AI workspace!*
