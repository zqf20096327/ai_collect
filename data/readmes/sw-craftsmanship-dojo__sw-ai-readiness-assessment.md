## What This Reveals

Four questions. Five minutes. One number that tells you where you stand.

| Dimension     | The Question                                                            |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Focus**     | Can your people focus enough to supervise AI output?                    |
| **Technical** | Can they challenge AI on correctness, security, and predictability?     |
| **Product**   | Is product intent clear enough to build the right thing?                |
| **Feedback**  | Do users and feedback correct you fast enough?                          |

Your answers produce an **AI Readiness Gap** — the distance between how fast you are adopting AI and how prepared your organization is to absorb that acceleration.

That gap now carries legal and financial risk.
