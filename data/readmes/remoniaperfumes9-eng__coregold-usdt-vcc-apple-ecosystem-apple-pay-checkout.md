## Practical Knowledge for Apple Wallet & App Store Users

### 1. Universal Apple Pay Compatibility
All CoreGold card BINs support binding to **Apple Pay** (as well as **Google Pay**), and the card selection screen also highlights dedicated categories for **Apple Pay / Google Pay** and **Google Play / Apple Store**.

### 2. Instant OTP Retrieval Inside CoreGold
When Apple Wallet prompts you for **email verification** during card setup, don't wait around in your external email client:
1. Enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually in Apple Wallet and request email verification.
2. Switch back to CoreGold **Card Management**, open that card's details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom—the live verification code appears right on screen.

### 3. Aligning Billing Details on Apple ID
If you also add the card to your US Apple ID payment settings, make sure the street address, city, state, and 5-digit ZIP code match your CoreGold card's billing record verbatim.

## Simple Step-by-Step Setup Tutorial

### Step 1: Register on CoreGold
Visit [https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b](https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b), register using your regular email address, and log in to the user dashboard.

### Step 2: Deposit USDT via TRON (TRC20)
1. In the left menu, click **My Wallet (我的钱包)** -> **Deposit (充值)**.
2. Enter your deposit amount to display the USDT deposit address and QR code.
3. Open your crypto wallet or exchange (such as OKX or Binance), select USDT withdrawal/transfer, and choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
4. Copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, verify the address, network, and amount, and confirm the transfer. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact customer support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### Step 3: Select Plan, Card Category & Billing Details
1. Click **Apply for Card (申请开卡)** in the left menu and choose an **Individual Plan** (such as Diamond Plan or AI Subscription) or **Team Plan**.
2. Pick the matching US-issued card BIN category and enter the initial deposit amount.
3. Check **Auto-Generate Billing Info** or uncheck it to enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keeping billing addresses consistent during checkout.

### Step 4: View Card & Bind Apple Pay
1. Open **Card Management (卡片管理)** and select your card to view card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, or use top-up, withdrawal, and freeze features.
2. To bind **Apple Pay**:
   - Open **Apple Wallet** on your iPhone, tap **`+`** -> **Add Payment Card**, and enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually.
   - Select **email verification** when prompted.
   - Return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code.
   - Enter the verification code back into Apple Wallet to complete binding.
