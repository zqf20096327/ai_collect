# Quantium Research

This repository contains quantitative trading strategies and research experiments implemented as Jupyter notebooks. The
goal of this public research is to demonstrate uncommon strategy ideas that I came up with or was inspired by a blog
post, article, video, or somewhere else. Each notebook explores different approaches to market analysis and prediction,
from traditional charting techniques to modern machine learning models.
