# Snowcase

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) | [日本語](README.ja-JP.md) | [한국어](README.ko-KR.md)

A local asset manager built for the BOOTH ecosystem. It also handles packages already on your disk and items bought from any other website. See what's inside every package, remember where you got it and where you use it, and put away what you no longer need. With AI by your side for every step of your VRChat avatar life, from shopping to customizing to uploading.

> **Status: early development.** Nothing is usable yet.

## Planned features

- **Library**: keep every asset in one place, whether it came from BOOTH, another store, an author's own website, or a folder on your disk.
- **Links and notes**: attach the source page, notes, and tags to any asset.
- **Package preview**: see what's inside a `.unitypackage` without opening Unity, including images and 3D models.
- **Item info**: fill in the title, author, images, and version from the item page.
- **License check**: read VN3 license terms and warn you about restrictions, such as public upload or redistribution.
- **Where it's used**: see which of your Unity projects and uploaded avatars use each asset, and which version.
- **VRCX-0 integration**: see the assets each of your avatars uses inside [VRCX-0](https://github.com/Map1en/VRCX-0).
- **Storage cleanup**: save disk space across package versions, archive what you don't use, and tidy up on a schedule.
- **BOOTH library sync**: download your own BOOTH purchases.

All data stays on your machine. Snowcase never uploads or shares asset files.

## Platforms

Windows first. macOS and Linux are planned.
