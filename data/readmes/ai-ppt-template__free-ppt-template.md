## Bulk download

`index.json` carries every template — title, description, tags, colors, the detail page, and a direct `.pptx` URL — so the whole catalog is scriptable:

```bash
# grab every minimalist business deck
jq -r '.[] | select(.tags | index("Business")) | select(.tags | index("Minimalist")) | .pptx_url' index.json | xargs -n1 -P4 curl -O

# list decks with at least 12 slides
jq -r '.[] | select(.slide_count >= 12) | .title' index.json

# every blue template's detail page
jq -r '.[] | select(.colors | index("blue")) | .url' index.json
```

## How these were made

Each deck is generated end to end: a language model writes the title, structure, and copy; an image model renders every slide as a full-bleed 16:9 design; the slides are packaged into an editable `.pptx`. Nothing is traced or re-hosted from a stock library, and no deck carries a watermark, logo, or attribution mark.
